# RemindMEE — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: 8 October 2026**  
**Last Updated: 9 October 2026**

RemindMEE ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered reminder application operated by **RemindMEE**, based in India.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share, store, and protect personal information when you use the RemindMEE Android application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information

We collect the email address or mobile phone number you provide when registering or signing in, along with account identifiers and authentication-related information.

### 1.2 Photos, Screenshots, and Documents

When you scan a photograph or screenshot, RemindMEE processes the selected image to extract information that may help create a reminder.

Images may contain names, addresses, financial information, identification details, prescription information, medicine instructions, or other personal data.

Selected images are transmitted to our backend and AI-processing providers. Photos associated with saved reminders may also be stored locally and in your account's cloud storage.

### 1.3 Reminder Information

We process reminder titles, dates, times, categories, notes, and extracted information, including amounts, expiry dates, and other fields you save.

### 1.4 Device Permissions

RemindMEE may request camera, microphone, and notification or alarm permissions to provide relevant features.

We do not currently use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

### 1.5 Purchase Information

Purchases are processed using Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.

We receive information needed to verify purchases, manage subscriptions, and allocate scan credits, including purchase identifiers and subscription status.

We do not directly collect payment card details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process information to:

- Create, manage, and synchronize reminders.
- Extract text and reminder details using OCR and AI.
- Authenticate users and secure accounts.
- Deliver or schedule reminders.
- Manage subscriptions, purchases, and scan allowances.
- Prevent fraud, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the service.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your photographs or document contents to advertisers.

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

RemindMEE currently uses:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Authentication, database, and photo storage |
| Google Cloud Vision | OCR processing of scanned photographs |
| Google Gemini | AI extraction using scanned photographs and OCR text |
| RevenueCat | Subscription and purchase management |
| Google Play Billing | Payment processing |
| Message Central | SMS verification and authentication |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Crash and error reporting (diagnostic data only — device model, OS version, app version, and crash/error logs; never document or photo contents) |

These services receive information necessary for their respective functions.

Third-party providers may have their own data-processing, retention, and security practices. We do not control every aspect of their infrastructure or retention practices.

We do not currently share document contents with advertising or analytics providers.

## 4. AI Processing and Accuracy

RemindMEE uses automated image recognition and artificial intelligence.

AI-generated reminder details may be inaccurate, incomplete, or incorrectly inferred.

Users must review and confirm important dates, times, amounts, medicine instructions, and other information before relying on reminders.

RemindMEE's reminders and notifications are a support tool only. Do not rely solely on the app's alerting mechanism for critical medical, legal, or financial obligations — always maintain an independent check or backup reminder.

RemindMEE is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Account information, reminders, and cloud-backed photographs are stored using Supabase.

Access controls are designed to restrict access to information associated with each authenticated user.

We apply reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee that any internet-based service is completely secure.

## 6. Photo and Reminder Deletion

When you delete an individual reminder, RemindMEE attempts to remove its associated photograph from your device and Supabase cloud storage.

**Important limitations:**

- Cloud deletion requires successful communication with the backend.
- If your device is offline or a cloud deletion request fails, the associated photograph may remain in cloud storage.
- The current version does not guarantee automatic retries of failed cloud-photo deletions.
- Removing a reminder does not necessarily mean that every associated copy, backup, or service-provider record has been immediately erased.

If you want to request deletion of remaining photographs or personal information, contact **remindmee9999@gmail.com**.

We will handle valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app (tap the **⋮** menu at the top-right of the home screen → **Delete account**), or by contacting **remindmee9999@gmail.com**.

Account deletion removes your account-associated cloud photographs, authentication records, and related application data (reminders, subscription records, usage history).

Deletion may be subject to legal retention obligations, technical limitations, and backup-retention periods.

We will process valid deletion requests within applicable legal time limits.

## 8. Data Retention

Reminder records and associated photographs may remain stored while an account exists.

Deleted reminders may have residual cloud photographs if deletion fails.

Some information may be retained in backups, transaction records, or service-provider systems according to applicable retention requirements.
