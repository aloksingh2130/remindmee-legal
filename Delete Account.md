# RemindMEE — Delete Your Account

**Last Updated: 9 October 2026**

This page explains how to delete your **RemindMEE** account (Android app on Google Play, operated by **RemindMEE**, India) and the data associated with it.

---

## How to delete your account

**Option 1 — In the app (instant)**

1. Open RemindMEE and sign in.
2. On the home screen, tap the **⋮** menu (top-right).
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

**Option 2 — By email**

Email **remindmee9999@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my account"**, sent from your registered email address (or include the phone number you signed in with). We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

---

## Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app, swipe a reminder left (or open it and tap **Delete**) to remove that reminder and its photo. You can also email **remindmee9999@gmail.com** to request deletion of specific data.

---

## What is deleted

- Your account and sign-in details (email address / phone number)
- All your reminders
- All photographs you uploaded and the details extracted from them
- Subscription and scan-usage records linked to your account

## What may be kept

- **Purchase records** held by Google Play and RevenueCat, as required for billing, refunds, and tax/legal obligations.
- **Crash reports** in Firebase Crashlytics, which contain only technical diagnostic data (no photos or reminder content) and are automatically deleted within 90 days.
- Data in routine backups, which is overwritten as part of the normal backup cycle.
